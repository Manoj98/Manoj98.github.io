01 About

I build and ship production AI systems, from LLM-powered agentic applications to the machine learning systems that power real-world products.

Over the past 6+ years, I've worked across AI/ML engineering, applied AI, and data science, taking systems from experimentation to production. I've designed multi-stage agentic workflows using Claude on AWS Bedrock, built retrieval and recommendation systems combining LLMs with hybrid semantic search, and developed the evaluation, monitoring, and responsible AI guardrails needed to deploy these systems reliably at scale. My production AI work currently supports 30K+ users.

Before GenAI, I built and deployed machine learning systems across fintech and insurance. At AB InBev, I developed XGBoost credit risk models and causal inference solutions that generated ~$9M in annual value and helped reduce SMB credit overdues by 30%. At Prudential, I've applied experimentation, personalization, propensity modeling, and AI/ML to large-scale customer-facing products.

What I care about most is turning AI from a prototype into a product that survives contact with real users: reliable, measurable, safe, and cost-efficient at scale.

Currently, I'm a Senior AI/ML Engineer at Prudential, building agentic AI products for a 30K+ member wellness platform. I'm also Deputy Chair of the AI/Data Analytics functional area for the LTCi Consortium, working across carriers to advance AI best practices.
